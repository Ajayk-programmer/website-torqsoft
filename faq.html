What is the hiring process at TorqSoft Solutions? ▼
1
Application Submission

Submit your application through our careers portal or job boards. Our HR team reviews all applications within 3-5 business days.

2
Initial Screening

Phone/video screening with HR to discuss your background, experience, and interest in the role (30-45 minutes).

3
Technical Assessment

Online coding test, technical quiz, or take-home assignment based on the role requirements.

4
Technical Interview

In-depth technical discussion with senior team members, including problem-solving and system design (1-2 hours).

5
Final Interview

Meeting with hiring manager and team leads to assess cultural fit and discuss career goals (45-60 minutes).

6
Offer & Onboarding

Reference checks, offer negotiation, and comprehensive onboarding program for successful candidates.

How long does the hiring process typically take? ▼

Our hiring process is designed to be thorough yet efficient:

  • Standard Process: 2-3 weeks from application to offer
  • Senior Positions: 3-4 weeks due to additional stakeholder interviews
  • Urgent Hiring: Can be expedited to 1-2 weeks for critical roles
  • Graduate Programs: 4-6 weeks including assessment centers

We keep candidates informed at every stage and provide feedback regardless of the outcome.
