# 🌐 Torqsoft Solutions Website

Welcome to the official repository for the **Torqsoft Solutions** website.  
This project is a **static website** built using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**, designed to represent our company online with modern design and user-friendly navigation.

---

## 📖 About Torqsoft
Torqsoft Solutions is an IT and consulting company focused on delivering **innovative technology solutions**.  
We specialize in **web development, mobile applications, cloud services, AI, and business process automation**, ensuring businesses can adapt to the future with ease.

---

## 📂 Pages & Features

### 🏠 Home
- Clean and professional landing section  
- Dynamic banner with engaging background  
- Quick navigation to Services, Career, and Contact  

### 💼 Services
- Web Development (Full-stack solutions)  
- Mobile App Development  
- Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning  
- Cloud Solutions & Cybersecurity  
- Consulting & Strategy  

### 🕰️ History
- Timeline-based design showing milestones from company inception to the present  
- Highlights of projects, growth, and partnerships  

### 👨‍💻 Career
- Job listings with categories (Engineering, Design, Consulting, etc.)  
- Application form (Name, Email, Resume upload, Cover letter)  
- Company culture highlights  

### 📞 Contact
- Interactive contact form with validation  
- Company email, phone number, and address  
- Embedded Google Map showing office location  

### 🖼️ Sample Images
- Showcase of team culture, projects, and achievements  


---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- **HTML5** → Semantic structure & accessibility  
- **CSS3** → Styling, animations, responsive design  
- **JavaScript (ES6)** → Interactivity and dynamic UI  
- **Google Fonts & Icons** → Modern typography and icons  
- **Responsive Design** → Mobile-first approach
