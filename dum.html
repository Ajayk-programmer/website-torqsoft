Our Story

Founded in 2025 by Sakthi Sundar, Aprajith, Ajay, and Prem from Saveetha Engineering College, TorqSoft Solutions Pvt Ltd represents the perfect blend of academic excellence and entrepreneurial spirit.

We are a cutting-edge technology and consulting firm that helps businesses, organizations, and individuals adapt to the future through innovative solutions and strategic insights.

Our team combines fresh perspectives with proven methodologies to deliver technology services, design & engineering, and strategic consulting tailored to your unique needs.
